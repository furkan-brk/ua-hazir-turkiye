# Judging

**The investment of a 20-person test jury decides the ranking: the team that receives the most investment wins.**

## Investment system

1. Only finalist projects that pass UA verification are uploaded to the shared system the organizers prepare.
2. Every test jury member receives **the same virtual balance** in the system.
3. After watching the live demos on final day, members invest their balance in any projects, in any amount they like. No one can exceed their own balance.
4. The ranking is set by the **total investment** each project receives.

In a tie, the team that received investment from more jury members wins.

## Test jury

A mixed group of 20: end users, developers, people from the domain name and email industry, and UA community representatives. The exact makeup will be announced once confirmed. Jury members cannot have a conflict of interest with finalist teams.

## Where does UA compliance come in?

Only projects that pass [verification](dogrulama.md) enter the investment round. UA compliance is therefore an **entry threshold**; among the projects that clear it, the jury's investment decides the winner. The jury also sees the live EAI test before deciding.

## Awards

- **Top three teams:** The 3 teams with the most investment receive prizes. Amounts will be announced once confirmed.
- **Participation certificate:** Given to every participant.
- **UA-Ready badge:** Given to all projects that pass verification.
- **Special awards:** Best open-source contribution, hardest UA problem solved, best student team.
- **Visibility:** Case studies are published and may appear in ICANN's UA Day report.

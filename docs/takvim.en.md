# Schedule

**From call to final takes about 6 weeks, about 3 of them for adaptation.** The dates below are examples; they fit ICANN's 25 March – 30 May event window, and the final is placed before the 3–13 May spring exam weeks.

<div class="tl" markdown>
<div class="tl-item" markdown>
<time>22 March – 1 April 2027</time>
<b>Call and applications</b>
<p>Projects apply.</p>
</div>
<div class="tl-item" markdown>
<time>2 – 4 April</time>
<b>Screening</b>
<p>Eligibility check and acceptance notice.</p>
</div>
<div class="tl-item" markdown>
<time>5 – 6 April</time>
<b>Kick-off and training</b>
<p>ICANN technical training and email system introduction.</p>
</div>
<div class="tl-item" markdown>
<time>5 – 23 April</time>
<b>Adaptation</b>
<p>Teams fix their projects; weekly mentor hours. Evidence is collected during this period.</p>
</div>
<div class="tl-item" markdown>
<time>24 – 28 April</time>
<b>UA verification</b>
<p>Evidence is submitted and the technical committee tests; one fix allowed.</p>
</div>
<div class="tl-item is-final" markdown>
<time>1 May</time>
<b>Final day</b>
<p>Awareness session, live demos, test jury investment round and awards ceremony.</p>
</div>
</div>

!!! note "Dates may change"
    Final dates will be announced after ICANN confirmation.

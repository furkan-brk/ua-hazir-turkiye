# How It Works

**You fix your existing project, pass the test to reach the final, and in the final the test jury invests in the projects it likes.**

## Who can take part?

Any software that accepts, processes or displays domain names or email addresses: websites, mobile apps, desktop software, plugins, libraries, APIs. Teams have up to 4 people; one person is fine too.

**The only requirement:** a working version when you apply. The project doesn't need to be finished or published online. The application form is [on the home page](index.md#basvuru).

## Support

Training, weekly mentor hours and Turkish-character test email addresses for every team. Every participant gets a **participation certificate**.

## How do you reach the final?

1. Pass the **required** items in the [checklist](kriterler.md).
2. Submit one piece of evidence (screenshot, test output) per item on 24 April.
3. The committee runs the same test for everyone. Projects that pass everything go to the final and get the **UA-Ready badge**.

An ICANN observer may join the tests; we don't wait for a result. The committee picks the finalists.

## How is the winner decided? { #kazanan }

1. Each of the 20 test jury members gets the same virtual balance.
2. After the live demos, the jury spreads its balance over the projects it likes, however it wants.
3. **The team with the most investment wins.** In a tie, the team that got investment from more people wins.

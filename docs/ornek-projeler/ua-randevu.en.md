# Appointment and booking system

<div class="concept-meta"><span class="badge cat">Web and E-commerce</span></div>

<div class="concept-lead" markdown>
Make reminder emails and cancellation links of a clinic, salon or restaurant booking system UA-ready.
</div>

**Who is it for?** Small businesses and service providers that use a booking system.

=== "UA touchpoints"

    - The email field in the booking form
    - Reminder and cancellation emails
    - Attendee address inside the calendar invite (.ics)
    - Admin panel search and filters

=== "Turkish test scenarios"

    - [ ] Book with `gülşen@sağlık.com`
    - [ ] Open the cancellation link from the email
    - [ ] Import the calendar invite and check the attendee

=== "Success criteria"

    - [ ] Booking, reminder and cancellation flows work with an EAI address
    - [ ] The address displays correctly in the calendar invite

**Suggested technology:** Next.js or Laravel, PostgreSQL, SMTP

# Verification

**Only projects that pass every required UA item reach the final.** Verification has two steps; ICANN observation runs alongside them.

## Steps

1. **Your own test:** You collect evidence for the required items in the [criteria list](kriterler.md) during adaptation and submit it at the start of verification week.
2. **Technical committee test:** The committee runs the same scenarios for every project. **Threshold: all required items must pass.** "Bonus" items do not affect the threshold.

A project that fails gets one chance to fix and resubmit. A project that passes is invited to the final and enters the investment round.

## ICANN observation

An observer from the ICANN UA team is requested. The observer accompanies the committee tests during verification week (if available). This observation is **not an approval we wait for**: finalists are selected by committee decision in verification week, and prizes are awarded on final day based on the investment result. ICANN only observes the process.

## UA-Ready badge

Projects that pass verification receive a **UA-Ready** badge they can use on their website and application page. The badge is issued in the organizers' name.

## Preparation tips

- Fix forms and validation rules first; most bugs are there.
- Check database column types and encoding (UTF-8).
- Test end to end with test addresses; sending and receiving real email exposes problems.
- Collect screenshots and test output during adaptation; don't leave it for the last day.
- Use the mentor hours.

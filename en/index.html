    Universal Acceptance Compliance Call · 2027

    Make your project recognize every address.¶

    Make your existing software support email addresses with Turkish characters, like şükrü@çiçek.com.tr. Fix it, reach the final, raise investment from the test jury.

    The only requirement: a working version when you apply.

    Apply How it works

    At a glance¶
    Who Any software that uses domain names or email. Teams of up to 4.
    Dates Applications 22 March – 1 April · Final 1 May
    Duration About 3 weeks of fixing, then a one-day final
    Winner The team with the most investment from the 20-person test jury
    Awards Prizes for the top 3 teams, a certificate for every participant

    4 steps¶

    Apply¶

    Fill in the form below.

    Fix¶

    Fix your project with training and mentor support.

    Pass the test¶

    Pass the checklist to reach the final.

    Raise investment¶

    Give a live demo and let the jury invest in what it likes.

    How is the winner decided?¶

    • Each of the 20 test jury members gets the same virtual balance.
    • Everyone spreads their balance over the projects they like, however they want.
    • The team with the most investment wins.

    Application form

    One application per team is enough. Keep it short; we'll go into detail later.

    Turkish-character addresses are fine.

    Frequently asked questions¶

    Do I need to write a project from scratch?

    No. A working project is enough. It doesn't have to be finished either.

    What is Universal Acceptance (UA)?

    Every piece of software accepting email addresses and domain names with Turkish characters without problems. Details on the FAQ page.

    Is there a certificate?

    Yes. Every participant receives a participation certificate.

    I don't know this topic. Can I apply?

    Yes. Training and mentor support exist for that.

    See the FAQ for other questions.
